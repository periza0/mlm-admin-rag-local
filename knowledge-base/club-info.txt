# MOHAN LAL MEMORIAL SPORTS CLUB (MLM)
## CUSTOMER KNOWLEDGE BASE

### 1. ABOUT THE CLUB

Mohan Lal Memorial Sports Club (MLM) offers badminton facilities, coaching, fitness support, physiotherapy support, accommodation for outstation players, a sports equipment shop, and a juice bar/canteen.

The club has four badminton courts.

### 2. BADMINTON COURTS AND FACILITIES

MLM Sports Club has four premium synthetic badminton courts.

The court setup has two layers:
- **Wooden subflooring by Jaipur:** Installed beneath the playing surface to provide extra cushioning and help reduce impact while playing.
- **Synthetic court surface by Transformer:** Installed above the wooden subflooring and used as the playing surface.

The club also provides strength and conditioning support, physiotherapy support, and sports science-based warm-up, cool-down, and recovery practices.

### 3. MEMBERSHIP WITHOUT COACHING

Membership is for players who want to use the badminton facilities without a coach.

Available membership durations:
- Monthly
- 3 months
- 6 months
- Annual

These plans are suitable for players who prefer to practise and play independently.

Membership prices and terms are not listed in this knowledge base. Customers should contact the club to confirm current rates and conditions.

Membership does not include coaching.

### 4. BADMINTON COACHING

Coaching is available for beginners to advanced players.

Training focuses on:
- Technical badminton skills.
- Match strategy.
- Player development.
- Regular performance and fitness assessments.

Coaching fees depend on the player's existing skill and knowledge level.

Customers should contact the club for coaching fees, schedules, and programme details.

### 5. HOURLY COURT BOOKING

Players can enquire about booking a badminton court by the hour.

Customers can book online through The District app or contact the club directly.

Court availability, booking charges, and available time slots may change. Customers should check the booking platform or contact the club for current information.

A chatbot response does not confirm a reservation.

### 6. SPORTS EQUIPMENT SHOP

MLM Sports Club has its own sports equipment shop.

The main product categories include:
- Badminton rackets.
- Shuttlecocks.
- Gym equipment and gear.
- Sports clothing.

Customers can contact the shop to enquire about product details, prices, and availability.

#### Preordering Products

If a product is not readily available, customers can enquire about preordering it.

A preorder is not confirmed until the shop confirms it directly.

#### Price Matching

The shop offers price matching.

If a customer finds the same product at a lower price online, they can contact the shop to enquire about matching the price.

Customers should share the product details and online price. The club will confirm whether the product and price qualify.

The chatbot must not guarantee a price match or invent eligibility conditions.

### 7. JUICE BAR AND CANTEEN

The club has a juice bar and canteen where players and visitors can get refreshments.

Menu items, prices, and operating hours are not specified in this knowledge base. Customers should contact the club for these details.

### 8. ACCOMMODATION

Residential accommodation is available for outstation players.

Both short-term and long-term stay options are offered.

Customers should contact the club to confirm accommodation availability, facilities, duration, and charges.

### 9. CUSTOMER QUESTION HANDLING

When answering questions about MLM Sports Club:

- Give a direct, helpful answer in simple English.
- Use only information supported by this knowledge base or approved club data.
- Do not invent prices, schedules, product availability, membership terms, or booking slots.
- If a customer asks for information that is not available, explain that it needs confirmation from the club.
- Do not claim that a booking, preorder, or price match has been confirmed.
- For phone numbers, booking links, and the club's location, use the fixed information stored in the backend rather than generating these details.
- If a question is unrelated to MLM Sports Club, politely explain that the assistant specialises in club information.
